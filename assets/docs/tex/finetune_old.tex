\section{Training an LLM for Discovery}
\label{sec:training}
% ============================================================

Everything so far treats $p_\theta$ as fixed and does the discovery work at inference time. The regret
decomposition of \S\ref{thm:regret} shows the LLM enters through exactly two quantities, the
search-suboptimality $\varepsilon_t$ (Assumption~\ref{ass:ucb-calibration}) and the reservoir coverage
$\kappa_t(\varepsilon)$ of \eqref{eq:coverage}, so \emph{training for discovery} means improving both. The
natural way to do so is to \emph{amortise} into the weights the very object the inference-time search
approximates: the tilted distribution $\pi_t$ of \eqref{eq:core}, whose reward is the calibrated acquisition
$\acq_t$. We sketch four routes, each a standard learning method specialised to this reward, and then the
economics that make them practical.

\subsection{Amortising the tilted policy}
\label{sec:amortise}
By Proposition~\ref{prop:gibbs}, $\pi_t\propto p_{\theta,\alpha}(\cdot\mid\C_t)\exp\{\eta\,\acq_t\}$ is the
optimal policy of a KL-regularised reward-maximisation problem with reward $\acq_t$, the control-as-inference
view of \citet{levine2018reinforcement}. Its fixed point is reached by reward-weighted regression: fine-tune on
self-generated designs reweighted by the exponentiated acquisition,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:amortize}
\max_{\theta}\ \mathbb{E}_{x\sim p_{\theta_{\mathrm{old}}}(\cdot\mid\C_t)}\big[\,e^{\eta\,\acq_t(x)}\,\log p_\theta(x\mid\C_t)\,\big],
\end{equation}
the expectation-maximisation step that contracts $p_\theta$ onto $\pi_t$ \citep{peng2019advantage}.
Equivalently, since $\pi_t$ is the closed-form optimum of KL-regularised policy optimisation, one may train
toward it directly from pairs ranked by the acquisition, as in direct preference optimisation
\citep{rafailov2023direct}, with $\acq_t$ supplying the preference signal in place of a human label. The reward
is the \emph{full} acquisition, including the exploration bonus and the novelty term of
\S\ref{sec:constrained}, not the posterior mean, so the amortised policy proposes high-value designs without
search, lowering $\varepsilon_t$, while keeping mass on uncertain and novel regions.

\subsection{Diversity-preserving training: coverage without collapse}
\label{sec:gflownet}
Plain reward maximisation concentrates $p_\theta$ on the current $\argmax$, which is the wrong objective for
discovery: it drives $\kappa_t(\varepsilon)$ on the \emph{unseen} part of $S_\varepsilon$ to zero. Discovery
needs a reservoir that samples \emph{proportionally} to value and covers many modes of the super-level set.
Generative flow networks train exactly such a policy, sampling terminal objects with probability proportional
to a reward $R(x)$ \citep{bengio2021flow}; taking $R(x)=\exp\{\eta\,\acq_t(x)\}$ makes the trained reservoir a
multi-modal sampler for $\pi_t$ by construction, and the same recipe has produced diverse biological sequence
designs \citep{jain2022biological}, the antibody and peptide setting of \S\ref{sec:cs-antibody-results}. A
flow-network reservoir raises the near-optimal mass $\kappa_t(\varepsilon)$ while preserving the breadth that a
temperature-sharpened or greedily fine-tuned model destroys, which is what keeps the discovery channel of
\S\ref{sec:decomp} open after training.

\subsection{Learning the self-improving property}
\label{sec:metatrain}
The adaptive condition \eqref{eq:adaptive}, that $\kappa_t(\varepsilon)$ \emph{rises} as $\C_t$ accumulates
good exemplars, was assumed in \S\ref{thm:regret}; it can instead be \emph{trained}. Meta-train the
LLM across many optimisation episodes whose context is a history of design--value pairs and whose target is the
next high-value design: the model learns to improve its own proposals from history, which is in-context
reinforcement learning by algorithm distillation \citep{laskin2022context} and its supervised-pretraining
variants. After such training, conditioning on good exemplars sharpens the proposal by construction, so
\eqref{eq:adaptive} holds as a learned property rather than an assumption, and the phase transition of
\S\ref{thm:regret}, from slow to geometric discovery, is built into the weights. Episodes can be drawn
cheaply from synthetic objectives, such as random draws of the GP prior or known benchmark families, with the
expensive $f$ reserved for calibration.

\subsection{Closing the loop: search as its own teacher}
\label{sec:selfdistill}
The inference-time search is a policy-improvement operator: it returns designs of higher acquisition than a
single sample from $p_\theta$. Distilling those back into $p_\theta$, by fine-tuning on the search's
high-acquisition outputs, is the expert-iteration recipe behind self-play agents \citep{silver2017mastering} in
its language-model form, reinforced self-training \citep{gulcehre2023reinforced}, and is the weight-space
counterpart of the inference-time self-refinement of \S\ref{sec:decomp}
\citep{madaan2023selfrefine,shinn2023reflexion}. Each round makes the next search cheaper (lower
$\varepsilon_t$) and the reservoir tighter (higher $\kappa_t$), so the loop bootstraps: every batch of
discoveries becomes training data that makes the next batch easier, the self-improving regime of
\S\ref{thm:regret} realised in the weights rather than only in context.

\subsection{What is trained against what}
\label{sec:trainecon}
Real evaluations are scarce, so almost all of the training signal in \eqref{eq:amortize} and its variants is the
\emph{surrogate} acquisition $\acq_t$, which is cheap and abundant, with the true $f$ reserved for recalibrating
the surrogate and validating final designs. This is model-based training: improve the policy against the
learned world model and touch the world sparingly, the training-time image of the model-based search of this
paper and of Dyna-style reinforcement learning. The calibration that makes $\acq_t$ a trustworthy inference-time
value (Assumption~\ref{ass:ucb-calibration}) is exactly what makes it a trustworthy training reward; an uncalibrated,
self-judged reward would be reward-hacked, the failure the surrogate exists to prevent. In this light, the
domain pretraining that \citet{3692070.3693095} require for a heavy near-optimal tail is simply the first
stage of the pipeline, how the reservoir acquires a small tail exponent $\nu$ before any online search begins.
